# Founder Visual Principles — Ñkyel AI

## Origin

The founder (INFJ-A profile) has a strong personal preference for global, relational, anticipatory, and visual understanding. He retains information better when he can see structure, connections, levels, and evolution in space.

This experience serves as **founding design intuition** — not as universal scientific proof or rigid user segmentation.

## Concrete Design Principles

### 1. Overview First
Show the whole picture before forcing detail reading. The mission view should always be one zoom-out away.

### 2. Visible Relationships
Make cause, dependency, contradiction, and proof relationships visible through typed edges in the Work Graph. Colors and line styles convey relationship type at a glance.

### 3. Semantic Zoom
Multiple levels of information density:
- **Level 0**: Goal, outcome, overall health
- **Level 1**: Major plan axes
- **Level 2**: Tasks, agents, sources, hypotheses
- **Level 3**: Metadata, tool calls, citations, versions, errors

Don't just shrink elements graphically — change the *amount* of information shown.

### 4. Spatial Stability
Maintain stable spatial positions to facilitate recall. A mission should feel like a familiar place when revisited. Don't randomly rearrange nodes between sessions.

### 5. Focus Mode
Offer a noise-free concentration mode showing only a single branch without visual clutter.

### 6. Mission as Place
Allow users to revisit a completed mission as a known location — the spatial layout becomes a memory aid.

### 7. Real Information Only
Every visual element must be tied to actual information or an actionable operation. No decorative animations pretending to show "AI thinking."

### 8. User Control
The user decides what to reveal, hide, move, or restart. Semantic edits (changing a title, rejecting a hypothesis) trigger replanification. Aesthetic moves (rearranging for clarity) do not.

### 9. Multimodal Accessibility
Always provide a text/outline alternative for the spatial view. The system must remain usable for people who prefer linear, textual presentation.

## What Ñkyel AI Is NOT

- Not "only for visual learners"
- Not a Myers-Briggs-based product
- Not claiming spatial cognition is superior

## What Ñkyel AI IS

A multimodal interface that makes complex problems more **comprehensible**, **controllable**, and **memorable** — through spatial reasoning as one powerful option among others.
